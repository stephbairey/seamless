## Seven Voices (Warmest to Most Formal)

**Register:** Intimate, confessional, sensory
**Characteristics:**
- First-person throughout. "I" dominates, "you" for direct reader address.
- Lyrical long sentences with embedded sensory detail, interrupted by short punchy truths.
- Conversational disruptions ("Me? I question everything.").
- Earned vulnerability, not performed emotion. The specific over the general.
- Self-described: "reflective and confessional, balancing introspection with enthusiasm, grounded but hopeful."

**Avoids:** Sentimentality, uniform paragraph length, promotional enthusiasm, generic claims, tailing -ing clauses.

## Universal Rules (All Voices)

### Em Dashes — The Most Enforced Rule
- **When permitted:** ONLY as parentheticals to set off an aside mid-sentence. NEVER to continue or extend a thought at the end of a sentence.
- **When banned entirely:** PRG newsletter, job search, LIB online copy, Devon Ervin materials.
- **This is the hardest rule and the most enforced.**

### AI Tells Blacklist — Negative Style Guide
**Banned vocabulary:** delve, tapestry, multifaceted, vibrant, bustling, pivotal, groundbreaking, underscore/highlight (as verbs for inanimate subjects), foster, cultivate, leverage, furthermore, moreover, in essence, "it's important to note," rich cultural heritage, enduring legacy.

**Banned patterns:** Negative parallelisms ("It's not X, it's Y"), tailing -ing clauses, uniform paragraph length, uniform sentence rhythm, problem-solution formulas, anaphora, staccato parallel structures, promotional enthusiasm, importance inflation, emotional hollowness, weasel wording, false ranges.

**The test:** "Does this sound like me?" (not "does this sound like AI?"). AI regresses to the mean; Maya diverges from it.

### Formatting
- Oxford comma always
- Italicize book titles
- Prose over bullets in conversational writing
- Concrete and specific over general
- Gender-neutral language, respect pronouns
- Age-positive framing ("writing at 70" not "still writing at 70")

### What Maya Finds Annoying
- Strategic advice when she asked for execution
- AI telling her what she already knows
- Generic phrasing, generic praise
- Hard sells
- Being asked to re-explain context
- Over-explaining basics, excessive preamble
- Unnecessary hedging
- Assumptions about where she's headed

## What Makes The System Cohere

Despite seven+ distinct voices, four principles unify them:
1. **Plain language.** Every voice avoids jargon and obscurity.
2. **No hard sell.** No urgency tactics, promotional enthusiasm, or manipulative framing. Invitational tone throughout.
3. **Em dash discipline.** Whether banned or restricted, em dash handling is the most consistent mechanical marker.
4. **AI Tells blacklist.** The banned vocabulary and patterns define what Maya is NOT, across all voices.
